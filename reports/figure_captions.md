## Figure 1. Share of wells with a reading, by season and year

Each cell is the percentage of the 2,759 wells measured in that round. Overall 86.4% of main-table rounds have a reading. ## Figure 9. Why High Stress exceeds the nominal 2.28%

(a) Observed High Stress rates: 6.38% (expanding), 6.90% (rolling) and 4.18% (adjusted), against 2.28% for a standard normal. The rolling baseline does not reduce the excess; the adjustment removes 54% of it. (b) With pure Gaussian noise (60,000 draws per n), the raw z exceeds 2 in 7.2% of cases at n = 5 and 3.1% at n = 22, while the adjusted score stays at 2.2-2.3%.

File(s): reports/figures/fig09_excess_high_stress.png; reports/tables/table09_monte_carlo_tail_rates.csv

## Figure 10. How many readings change category under other thresholds

Compared with the default thresholds (1 / 1.5 / 2 SD), lenient (0.5/1/1.5) changes 39,031 readings (23.47% of classified); strict (1.5/2/2.5) changes 24,413 readings (14.68% of classified); wide (1/2/3) changes 14,092 readings (8.47% of classified). Changes are one-directional per set, so thresholds shift severity but never reorder readings.

File(s): reports/figures/fig10_threshold_sensitivity.png; reports/tables/table10_threshold_sensitivity_*.csv

## Figure 11. Readings excluded by the minimum-history rule

Readings with fewer than K earlier same-season readings get no baseline, anomaly or stress label. The chosen K = 5 excludes 52,952 of 219,258 readings (24.15%); K = 3 would exclude 14.49% and K = 8 38.64%. The excluded readings are each well-season's first few, so labels effectively start around 2005.

## Figure 12. Rainfall climatology and an example well

(a) Mean monthly CHIRPS rainfall at the wells of five states. Most states peak in Jun-Sep, but Tamil Nadu gets 41% of its rain in Oct-Dec against 40% in Jun-Sep, which motivated the Oct-Dec features. (b) Example well W42fd920d92 (Pimpri (kh) malvasti, Maharashtra, 85 readings): the water table rises after each monsoon; the Pearson correlation between the 6-month rainfall window and depth is -0.36 (more rain, shallower water).

## Figure 13. Which data feed the features of one reading

For well W42fd920d92's November 2015 reading, rainfall windows end in October 2015 (M-1): the 1-, 3-, 6- and 12-month windows and the monsoon-year-to-date total (Jun-Oct 2015) use only blue months; November 2015 and later are never used. Its seasonal baseline uses the 14 earlier November readings (2000-2014) of the same well (mean 4.46 m, std 1.16 m), and lag features use only earlier rounds. Assertions and perturbation tests enforce this for every row.
